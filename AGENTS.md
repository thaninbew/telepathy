# Telepathy

- Build a native, local-first macOS gaze-focus utility in Swift.
- Prefer Apple frameworks and public APIs. Keep tracking data on device.
- Optimize for imperceptible latency without allowing focus thrash.
- Treat physical mouse movement and the emergency shortcut as authoritative.
- Keep the MVP dependency-free and cover deterministic logic with unit tests.
- Do not claim camera or Accessibility behavior without testing it on macOS.

# Telepathy logo exploration

These are original, one-color SVG studies for issue #5. They are design
proposals, not shipping assets.

## Contract

- Recognizable at the true 18×18 macOS menu-bar size.
- One color, no gradients, text, container, or baked-in background.
- Usable as a template image and as an accent-colored panel mark.
- Product meaning comes from attention moving between displays, not a generic
  surveillance eye.

## Concepts

- **Glance:** the most literal and immediately legible. It is also the least
  distinctive because eye outlines are common across icon systems.
- **Relay:** two peripheral arcs and one displaced focus point. It preserves an
  eye-like gestalt while removing the full eye outline and screen brackets.
- **Threshold:** two display edges and a focus point crossing context. It is the
  clearest product metaphor, but less immediately recognizable as gaze.

**Recommendation:** Relay. It uses three primitives, holds together at 18 px,
and communicates both peripheral attention and cross-display movement without
repeating the crowded eye-plus-brackets construction.

## Reference systems

Lucide, Phosphor, and Apple SF Symbols were reviewed only for small-size stroke
discipline and platform conventions. No path geometry was copied. The three
marks in this folder were drawn specifically for Telepathy.

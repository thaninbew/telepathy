# Telepathy logo exploration

These are original, one-color SVG studies for issue #5. They are design
proposals, not shipping assets.

## Contract

- Recognizable at the true 18×18 macOS menu-bar size.
- One color, no gradients, text, container, or baked-in background.
- Usable as a template image and as an accent-colored panel mark.
- Product meaning comes from attention moving between displays, not a generic
  surveillance eye.
- Distinctive through silhouette and negative space, not decorative detail.

## Round two: character marks

- **Cyclops:** a single-eyed monolith with an eye cut through one solid body.
  It is the simplest and most ownable silhouette of the three.
- **Sentinel:** a faceted optical guardian with a more technical posture. It is
  crisp at small sizes, but may suggest a security product.
- **Watcher:** an asymmetrical peripheral eye-creature. It has the strongest
  personality, but is the least formal direction.

**Recommendation:** Cyclops. It reduces the brand to one memorable character,
works as a filled macOS template image, and stays legible at 18 px.

## Reference boundary

Apple and GitHub were considered only as examples of proprietary filled
silhouettes. Greek Cyclops mythology and Terraria's Eye of Cthulhu were used
only as thematic prompts for a one-eyed character. No path geometry, character
features, or trademarked composition was copied.

## Round one: icon marks

The `glance.svg`, `relay.svg`, and `threshold.svg` files preserve the first
round. They remain useful evidence for what not to ship: balanced outline
strokes and obvious primitives made them feel like an icon set rather than a
brand.
